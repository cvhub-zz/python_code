## 🕹️ The Games in this Arcade

### 1. ⚔️ `quest_rpg_game.py` (The Dragon's Shadow RPG)
A full text adventure RPG featuring:
- **Player & Enemy Dictionaries**: Tracking HP, Attack, Level, and Gold.
- **Backpack List**: Storing weapons, shields, and potions.
- **Spellbook Functions**: `attack()`, `heal()`, `shop()`, and `battle()`.
- **Achievement Badges Set**: Unlocking unique accolades like "Dragon Slayer".
- **Game Loop**: Explore forests, dungeons, find treasure, and battle the Red Dragon!

### 2. 🐾 `virtual_pet.py` (CyberPet Tamagotchi Simulator)
An interactive virtual pet simulator featuring:
- **Status Meters**: Hunger, Happiness, Energy, and Cleanliness.
- **Interactive Menu**: Feed, Play Minigames, Teach Tricks (stored in a list/set), and Put to Bed.
- **Dynamic ASCII Art**: Your pet reacts with happy, sleepy, or hungry faces!

---

## 🚀 How to Play & Mod the Games

1. Run the games directly in PyCharm or Terminal:
   ```bash
   python3 lessons/09_capstone_arcade/quest_rpg_game.py
   python3 lessons/09_capstone_arcade/virtual_pet.py
   ```
2. **Modding Challenges (Make them your own!)**:
   - Add new monster types or rare weapons to `quest_rpg_game.py`.
   - Add a "Pet Evolution" feature to `virtual_pet.py` when Happiness reaches 100!
   - Invent new spells or items in the shop!
